Compare the War Room with your Sleeper claims

A bookmark you tap while you're on sleeper.com. It shows each pending claim next to the model's numbers: your bid and drop, the model's bid, likely price, value and your exact chance of winning at your bid. It flags drops used twice, bids above value, drops of a starter, and the model's suggestions you haven't claimed.

Private and read-only. Your claims are only visible to you, so they can't go on this public site. The bookmark reads them inside your own signed-in sleeper.com tab and shows them there. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere, and it never changes a claim.

The bookmark

War Room vs my claims

Set it up on a computer (Chrome)

  1. Show the bookmarks bar: press Ctrl+Shift+B (Mac: Cmd+Shift+B).
  2. Drag the orange War Room vs my claims button above onto the bookmarks bar.
  3. Open https://sleeper.com (signed in) and click War Room vs my claims on the bookmarks bar. A dark panel opens with your claims; Close is top right.

Set it up on a phone

Phone browsers can't drag a bookmark, so you save any page as a bookmark and paste the code in as its address. It works in the browser at sleeper.com, not in the Sleeper app.

  1. Tap Copy the code above.
  2. Bookmark this page (iPhone Safari: Share → Add Bookmark; Android Chrome: the star in the ⋮ menu).
  3. Edit that bookmark: name it War Room vs my claims and replace its address with the copied code. Save.
  4. Open https://sleeper.com in the same browser, signed in. Then open the bookmark (Safari: Bookmarks; Android Chrome: type War Room in the address bar and tap the bookmark suggestion).
