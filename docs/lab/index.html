Main page

A · Scoreboard is the main page (picked 2026-10-31). B, C and D stay one tap away in the ▦ layout menu at the top of every page. New ideas get tried here first; tap 👍 or 👎 (or 💬 Feedback) to say what to keep, drop or change.

AScoreboard

A tile per league: projected score, a bar, fix/move/add counts and the single most important move. Tap a tile and the full league slides in.

  • Best for: seeing all 8 leagues at once on a laptop
  • Phone: tiles stack, KPIs on top
Open A (main page)

BCommand center

One ranked inbox of everything to do across all leagues and the pools (fixes first, then picks, moves, adds, trades), with filters. Leagues in a side rail, scoreboard on the right.

  • Best for: "what do I need to do right now?"
  • Phone: rail becomes a chip bar
Open B

CDeck

Each league is a full-height card. On the phone you swipe between them; on a laptop they sit side by side as columns that scroll on their own. Sections fold open and shut.

  • Best for: flicking through leagues one by one
  • Laptop: 3–4 leagues visible side by side
Open C

DGame day

Everything on a clock: "Do first" on top, then every one of your starters across all leagues grouped by kickoff, each tagged with the leagues he starts in. Scoreboard alongside.

  • Best for: Sundays and NBA nights
  • Shows who you're riding on in each window
Open D

How feedback works

  1. Tap 👍 / 👎 on any tile or section, or 💬 Feedback, and add a note if you like.
  2. Send via GitHub opens a pre-filled GitHub issue. Tap Create (you're already signed in to GitHub).
  3. Within a minute it's logged in FEEDBACK.md, the issue closes itself, and the next Claude session and the monthly review act on it.
