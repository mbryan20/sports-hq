How it works

Data. League rosters, weekly scores, budgets and every waiver bid (won and lost) come from Sleeper. Player points come from nflverse weekly stats through week 7 including Monday night, scored 0.5 PPR. The bye schedule and Out/IR status come from nflverse rosters and injury reports.

Projections. Each player's weekly projection blends his season average with his last 3 games (the slider). A player is set to 0 on his bye, 0 for week 8 if ruled Out, and 0 through week 11 if on IR. Each team's weekly score is its best legal lineup: QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, TE, 2 FLEX, K, DEF.

Elimination. Each simulated week adds random noise (the swing slider) to every team's projection and cuts the lowest. Your expected weeks survived and odds of winning come from thousands of simulated seasons.

Bids. A player's value to you is how many extra weeks he adds to your expected survival, times the $ per week you set. The price estimate checks each rival: if the player would start for them, they are assumed to bid up to their boldest past share of budget (times the aggression slider), capped by what they have left.

Limits. These are simple projections from real 2026 results, not expert rankings. They miss role changes, trades and matchups, and the sim doesn't re-assign freed players. Use them to compare options, not as exact numbers.
