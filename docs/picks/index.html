How to read the CHANGE lines
  • CHANGE: yours X → card Y: the pick you saved on the pool site (X) differs from today's card (Y). Nothing changes until you change it on the pool site.
  • Pool -13.5 vs market -16.5: the pool fixed its line when it posted the games; the betting market's line today is in the second number. When the market moved past the pool's line, the pool is giving you free points (here 3).
  • cover 59%: the chance the pick wins at the pool's line, from the market line plus a small model adjustment. 50% is a coin flip; 55%+ is worth a pick.
  • Market Oklahoma 56%: the betting market's chance that team wins outright.
  • 80% of group on Ole Miss: the share of the Yahoo pool picking that side. In a pool you need to beat other players, so a slight favourite most of them fade gains you the most when it wins.
  • dropped: ... market -14: why your saved pick lost its edge (news, then the line it moved to).
  • Win % model/mkt (tables): our stats model's chance / the betting market's. The card leans on the market, which picked more winners in our backtest (75% vs 70%).
