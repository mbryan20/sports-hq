# Pool rules (read from the pool sites, Oct 26, 2026)

## BTO "Beat the Odds" — beattheoddspickem.com
- 16 games/week, college + NFL, against the spread; some games may be over/under. Even money.
- Wager at least 1/4 of balance (Three Bet: 50%; One Bet: 100%), up to full balance; $10 multiples; largest bet ≤ 2× smallest.
- 4.0 Line Points per week, spread across games however you like.
- Big Game must be among your picks (except One Bet).
- Desperate Times: Three Bet (≤ $1,000 or ≤ 50% of leader) = exactly 3 games incl. Big Game, ≥ 50% wagered. One Bet (≤ $500 or ≤ 25% of leader) = 1 game, 100%.
- Deadline typically before the week's first game; each game locks at kickoff. Missed week = Monkey picks.
- Top 12 at end of regular season make the playoffs (seeds 1–4 get byes; best-of-5 series, no wagers).
- Note: the setup brief said "must bet the full balance"; the site says minimum 1/4 (50% in Three Bet). Cards follow the site.

## CFP 2026 points pool — ncaa-pool-two.vercel.app
- Start 10,000 points; balance carries over. Commissioner posts games + spreads (normally by Tuesday); spreads are fixed for the week.
- Pick sides against the spread, any number of games; total wager 50–100% of current balance. Win = +wager, loss = −wager.
- Locks 11:00am CT Saturday. No picks = lose 50%.
- One $25 rebuy to 10,000, only immediately after hitting 0; none after 11am CST Nov 7.
- Leaderboard ranks by balance.

## Yahoo College Football Pick'em — Kosmos 2026, group 3659 (14 members)
- 25 games/week, straight-up winners. Each game locks at kickoff.
- TB1: exact scores of 2 designated games. TB2: team with most and fewest points among the 50 teams (locks before the week's first game).
- Season runs Sep 1 – Dec 5.
