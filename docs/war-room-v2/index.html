Who to bid on this week

Freed by the week ? cut (), plus the best free agents. Sorted by how much each player raises your odds of winning the league, after the drop he needs.

Bid options lists the amounts worth comparing for each player on waivers: the bids that won about 25%, 50% and 80% of comparable claims in this league, his full value to you, and the recommended bid (★). For each: the chance it wins, and the average payoff = chance × (his value − the bid), i.e. what that bid is worth to you on average. Bidding more wins more often but leaves less of his value; a negative payoff means overpaying. ★ is the amount with the highest average payoff, so it's often below the likely price: the aim is to spend wisely, not to win every claim. Each claim has its own drop. Free agents cost $0 once waivers clear. This page simulates 6,000 seasons so the controls respond quickly; the bids file the Tuesday checks read uses 20,000, so amounts can differ by a dollar or two.

How it works

Data. League rosters, weekly scores, budgets and every waiver bid (won and lost) come from Sleeper's API. Past player points come from nflverse (Sleeper's own stats fill in a week nflverse hasn't posted). Rebuilt on GitHub several times a day.

Projections. Sleeper's weekly projections for every remaining week, scored with this league's settings. They already put a player at 0 on his bye and when he's ruled out, so bye weeks (upcoming or already taken) count in every number here. Tested on this league's weeks 3–8, they missed real team scores by about 16 points a week, against about 20 for any mix of season and recent averages. Each team's weekly score is its best legal lineup: QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, TE, 2 FLEX, K, DEF.

Elimination. Each simulated week adds random noise (the swing) to every team's projection and cuts the lowest. The swing is measured from how far real scores landed from projections, and grows by about 1¼ points per week further out. Odds come from thousands of simulated seasons. A point in a near week counts more than one late in the season, because you have to survive the near week to use the late one.

Value. The league is winner-take-all, so a player is worth the odds of winning he adds, after dropping your least useful player. That is turned into dollars at the "$ per point of win odds" rate.

Bids. The model guesses the top rival bid (rivals who'd start him, scaled by their boldest past bid as a share of budget). That guess is checked against every past claim to get the chance a given bid wins. Your bid is the amount with the best chance × (value − bid). All bids together stay under FAAB minus the reserve, and no two share a drop.

Limits. These are simple projections from real 2026 results, not expert rankings. They miss role changes, trades and matchups, and the sim doesn't re-assign freed players. Use them to compare options, not as exact numbers.
