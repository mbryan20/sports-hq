# Q-071 · Score the picks Michael changed from the card

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-19. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
Model health compares two records that don't cover the same picks. `sections.picks.details` (Nov 19, 08:55Z):
- card 57-30 (`picks.card_ats` 0.655, n=87, weeks 9-11), entered 67-49 (`entered_rate` 0.578, n=116, weeks 8-11).
- Week 8 had no posted card (entered 14-15: Yahoo 12-13, BTO 1-2, CFP 1-0), so the entered rate takes in a week the
  card can't be judged on. Weeks 9-11 only: entered 53-34 (0.609) vs card 57-30 (0.655), the same 87 games.
- The gap is entirely where Michael changed a pick. `picks/pp/grade.py` already stores the changed picks in each
  postmortem's `differences`: 7 in weeks 9-11 (week 11 had none). The card's side went 6-1; the entered side 2-5.
  Week 9 BTO: MIN@DET (both won), Northwestern@Oregon (card won, entered lost); week 9 Yahoo: Illinois@Maryland,
  Northwestern@Oregon, Washington St.@SDSU (card won, entered lost each); week 10 Yahoo: Texas@Missouri (card won,
  entered lost), Fresno St.@Utah St. (card lost, entered won).
- Nothing reports this: health.json has no check on it, and the weekly wrap-up (`shq/wrapup.py` ~613) shows only how
  many differences there were, not how they turned out.
Seven picks is far too few to say overriding the card is a mistake; the point is to start counting it so he can see it.

## The change (`shq/health.py` only)
1. `picks_health`: also tally, over every postmortem's `pools.*.differences`, the card side (`card_result`) and the
   entered side (`entry_result`) as W-L-P, overall and per pool (bto / cfp / yahoo, since Yahoo is straight-up and the
   other two are against the spread). Also compute `entered_rate` over weeks with a posted card only (a pool-week
   whose `card.picks` is empty is skipped), so it lines up with `card_ats`.
2. Two new info rows: `picks.override_card` ("When you changed a pick: the card's side won") and
   `picks.override_entered` ("... your side won"), n = graded differences. Info only (no baseline, no flag) until
   n >= 20; then `picks.override_entered` becomes a "higher" check against `picks.override_card` with a 0.10
   tolerance (watch when his side trails the card's by 10+ points).
3. Note line: "Changed picks, weeks 9-11: 7; card's side 6-1, yours 2-5 (yours vs the card: BTO 1-1 vs 2-0, Yahoo 1-4 vs 4-1). Entered
   picks on weeks with a card: 53-34 vs the card's 57-30."
No page, push or card change. The scorecard (Q-013, when built) and the wrap-up can read the same numbers later.

## Test before shipping

  entered 53-34 on card weeks; `picks.card_ats` and the other picks rows unchanged.
- No out-of-sample model claim is made: these are counts of what happened.
