# Q-011 · One guillotine model: the dashboard reads War Room v2

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-10. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
Two models answer the same questions. `shq/guillotine.py` `analyze()` feeds the Guillotine tab, the Today tile, the 8am
digest line and the cut-risk push (`run.guillotine_odds`, `digest.standing_item`, `digest.danger_push`). `warroom/model.js`
feeds the War Room v2 page, `bids.json`, the Tuesday bid check and the AI write-up. They differ in swing (per-player
position SDs, about 27 a team, vs the measured 22), prices (boldest past bid share + $1 vs the calibrated ladder) and
value (points gained vs win odds).

Numbers (health.json, Nov 10 08:55Z):
- Cut odds as shown before kickoff, week 9 (MoneyManChas cut): v2 gave him 32.1% and ranked him 1st; the dashboard
  ranked him 2nd. Log-loss 1.136 (v2, n=1) vs 2.683 (dashboard, weeks 8-9, n=2); a coin toss scores 2.35.
- Prices, week 8 waiver run: dashboard missed by $12.4 (`gd.dash_price_mae`, n=8), v2 by $8.3 (`rec.est_mae`, n=6).
- Tonight they disagree on what I can do: the tab lists Harold Fannin Jr., Pat Freiermuth, Brenton Strange and
  T.J. Hockenson as "priced out: rivals may go $3-4, worth $0 to you"; v2 calls Strange, Freiermuth and Tyler Higbee
  "Add free" (waivers are closed until Wed Nov 11, so `analyze()` is pricing claims nobody can make).
Small samples (1-2 weeks, 1 waiver run): enough to pick a direction, not to call the dashboard model wrong.

## The change
One source before kickoff (v2), the live model only while games are being played.
1. `shq/guillotine.py`: new `from_v2(bids, league, now)` that returns the same dict shape as `analyze()` (week, n, rank,
   gap, proj, cut, survive, faab, waivers, teams, rich, bids, freed), filled from `bids.json`: `cut_risks` → teams
   (cut, proj, faab, bold); `me` → cut, proj; `bids[]` → the Bids list (name, pos, team, gain = `gain_next_week`,
   price = `est_price`, max = `your_max`, call lower-cased, plus `bid` and `win_chance`); `freed_players` → freed.
   Team names: keep the dashboard's (Sleeper team name), mapped by roster as `health.shown_cut_odds` already does.
2. New `choose(league, ctx, now, bids)`: use `from_v2` when bids.json is for this week (`week == ctx.week`), its cut is
   confirmed, and no game of the week has kicked off; otherwise `analyze()` as today (it locks started players at
   their real points; v2 can't until Q-002). The result carries `source: "v2"` or `"live"`.
3. `shq/run.py` `guillotine_odds`: load `docs/war-room-v2/bids.json` and call `choose`. `league.standing` (digest line,
   danger push) follows, so every screen and push shows v2's number before kickoff.
4. `docs/lib/core.js` (owned by the dashboard session): `bidText` learns two calls, "add free" ("Add free: <name>,
   +0.4 pts of win odds") and "coin flip" with the bid amount; the basis line says "Model: War Room v2" or "live".
   Without this step the tab still renders (same fields), it just words free adds as bids at $0.
What changes on screen: the tab's cut risks and Bids list match the War Room page and the Tuesday push; free agents
show as "Add free" when waivers are closed; on game days nothing changes.

## Test before shipping (out of sample)
- Unit: `from_v2` output passes the shape assertions in `tests/test_guillotine.py` and `tests/test_digest.py`; a stale
  file (wrong week, unconfirmed cut, or after kickoff) falls back to `analyze()`.
- Evidence gate: ship the switch only if, over at least 4 weeks scored as shown (weeks 9-12, health run of Tue Dec 1),
  `gd.v2_skill` is above `gd.dash_skill` and above 0; and over at least 3 waiver runs `rec.est_mae` stays below
  `gd.dash_price_mae`. Today: 1 week and 1 run, both in v2's favour.
- After shipping, health keeps scoring `analyze()` in the background (it still runs on game days) so the comparison
  continues; add `gd.shown_skill` for what the tab actually showed.

## Depends on

  week 9 while the dashboard was on week 10; without on-time builds step 2 would fall back to `analyze()` most of
  Tuesday morning, which is safe but defeats the point.
- Q-002 (v2 uses games already played) would remove the game-day fallback and let `analyze()` retire.

## Risks and rollback
- The cut-risk push (over 20%) changes source: on Nov 3 the two models gave MoneyManChas 17.0% vs 21.5%, so a push can
  fire in one and not the other. v2's narrower swing gives bolder odds; Q-012 covers whether that is right.
- A broken or late bids.json: `choose` falls back, so the tab is never empty.
- Rollback: `guillotine_odds` calls `analyze()` directly again (one line); `from_v2` is additive.
