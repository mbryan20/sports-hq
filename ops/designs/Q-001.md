# Q-001 · Denial value: what a freed star is worth when a rival gets him

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-13. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
War Room v2's simulation keeps every rival's current roster (`simulate(BASE.projs, ...)`), so a freed star Michael doesn't
claim never strengthens anyone. The league is winner-take-all: a star going to the strongest rival lowers Michael's title
odds even when he survives. warroom/README.md "Known limits" still lists it after the Nov 12 revamp. Not measured.

## Step 1: measure (a one-off script, no change to bids)
For each past waiver run in history (weeks 1-9, the cases `warroom_prices` builds) and the top 5 freed players by
projection: give the player to the rival who actually won him (from `recs.details.winners_paid`), with that rival's
best drop (`bestDrop(t, s)`), set `projs[t] = teamProj(t, [s], drop)`, and rerun `simulate` at 20,000 seasons with the
same seed. Record the change in Michael's title odds vs the unchanged rosters. Report: the median and largest drop, and
how often it exceeds the noise floor (`MIN_WIN`, 0.3 points). Write it to `reviews/` and the item's Evidence.

## Step 2: only if step 1 finds drops above the floor in 20%+ of top-5 players
In `gains()`, value a claim as (Michael wins him) vs (his likeliest rival wins him), weighted by the rival model's
chances (`rivalOdds`): denial = sum over rivals of P(rival wins him) x (Michael's odds with him on that rival - odds
with rosters unchanged). Add it to the claim's dWin, labelled in `why_line` ("+X keeps him from <rival>").
What Michael sees: higher values (and so bids) on stars the top rivals need; possibly a claim on a player who adds
little to his own lineup.

## Test out of sample before shipping
- Q-062's replay (weeks 2-9, prices fitted on earlier weeks only): title-odds points per $ spent must not fall below the
  revamp's +12.1 for $26. Spending must stay within his reserve and big-bid rules.
- Unit test: a freed player who only helps the leader shows a positive denial value; one nobody needs shows 0.

## Risks and rollback
- Inflates bids late in the season; his rules.json caps (`big_bid_share`, league-winners list) still bind.
- Build time: one extra simulation per claim per likely rival; cap at the top 3 rivals by claim chance.
- Rollback: a `denial` switch in rules.json, off.
