# Q-036 · NBA radar flags only rising roles that are worth a roster spot

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-06. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
The Nov 5 health run turned both radar checks to drift: `radar.held` 0.77 (17 of 22 flagged free agents kept the bigger
role; baseline 1.0 on 5 flags) and `radar.edge` 0.15 z per game (flags −2.51 vs the old suggestions −2.66; baseline 1.32 on
5 flags). A replay tonight (games through period 17) gave 25 flags, −2.53 vs −3.62: the check moves ~0.8 in a day at this n,
so part of the drift is a baseline set on 5 players. The real gap: `radar.rising()` looks only at minutes and usage. 16 of
the 25 flags projected under −3 z per game in the league's categories even at their new minutes; that projection
correlates 0.31 with what they did after. On periods 13-14, flags projected at −3 or better averaged −0.04 after (14
flag-mornings), those below −3 averaged −3.62 (23). The health check also still compares with "the 3 most-owned free
agents", which the site stopped suggesting when Q-028 shipped (value-ranked swaps, 168a299).

## The change
1. `shq/radar.py`: new `proj_value(sc, p, rl, cats)` = the sum of `cat_z` over the league's categories at his new minutes
   (`projected(p, rl)`; reversed categories negative), the same number `radar_backtest.value` uses after the fact.
   `block()` keeps a rising **free agent** only when `proj_value >= VALUE_FLOOR` (start at −3.0, set in step 4) or he helps a
   category I'm losing or close by z ≥ 1.0 (`fit_score` helps), so a blocks or 3PM specialist still shows. Bench risers are
   unchanged (already mine). Each riser line adds "value at his new minutes −1.2/g".
2. `send_alerts()` pushes only gated free agents (same one-push-a-day limit, PUSH_OWNED and PUSH_STRENGTH unchanged).
3. `shq/radar_backtest.py` `run()`: apply the same gate (a `floor` argument, None = today's behaviour) and change the
   comparison list to the top 3 free agents by value per game from the games before that morning (the nearest replay of
   Q-026/Q-028's ranking; the exact Q-028 swap of that morning isn't stored). Keep the % owned list as an info number.
4. Before shipping, set VALUE_FLOOR from the replay (see the test) and record the choice in ROADMAP "Lessons".
5. `shq/health.py`: `radar.edge` keeps its id and floor 0.0, compared with the new list; label "Radar flags' value over
   the site's value-ranked free agents (z per game)". After the build, Michael OKs resetting the `radar.held` and
   `radar.edge` baselines with n ≥ 20 (the pass never touches `state/health_baselines.json`).
What changes on the page: fewer "rising" free-agent lines in both basketball tabs, each with its projected value; the
radar push fires less often. bids.json and football are untouched.

## Test out of sample before shipping
- Pick VALUE_FLOOR on mornings up to Nov 5 (periods ≤ 14) from −5, −4, −3, −2 by the gated flags' mean value after.
- Measure it on later mornings only (periods 15+; enough games after them about Nov 10): the gated flags must beat the new
  comparison list by ≥ 0.5 z per game (`radar.edge` ≥ 0.5) with ≥ 10 flags, `radar.held` ≥ 0.75, and keep at least half of
  the flags whose role held.
- `tests/test_radar.py`: a low-value riser is dropped, a one-category specialist in a losing category is kept, bench
  risers unaffected; the backtest with `floor=None` matches today's numbers.
- If the later mornings don't clear 0.5, don't ship the gate: ship only step 3's comparison fix and re-check in December
  (the radar review's own advice).

## Risks and rollback
- Too few flags on quiet days: fine (ideas only), but watch that the radar push doesn't go silent for weeks; the health
  `flagged` count shows it.
- ESPN's early-season per-minute rates are noisy for players with few minutes before the jump (Josh Okogie, 6 min before).
  The gate uses this season's per-minute rates; if that hides true breakouts, blend toward ESPN's projection like Q-026.
- Rollback: VALUE_FLOOR = None (one constant); the comparison change is health-only.
