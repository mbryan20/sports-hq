# Q-040 · An injured or IR player with no projection still gets "Add free"

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-11. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
`warroom/model.js` `decideAll`, the free-agent branch (line 206), calls any free agent "Add free" when his rest-of-season
win odds clear `MIN_WIN` (0.003). It never asks whether he can play soon. bids.json `d8b4c87` (Tue Nov 10 9:26pm CT,
the last build before the Wed Nov 11 waiver run): Lamar Jackson (QB, injury "Out", `ir` true, `proj_next_week` 0,
`gain_next_week` 0) is "Add free", drop Woody Marks (RB, 8.2 projected), with the largest `extra_win_odds` of any row
(0.018; next Harold Fannin 0.016). The gain is real in the model: Sleeper projects him back later and he beats Jayden
Daniels in some weeks. What's missing is the label and the price of the wait: the page reads like a normal pickup, with
no "he is out until week N", and the model doesn't know whether my roster has an open IR slot (`warroom/model.py` reads
no `roster_positions` or `reserve`; not confirmed whether this league has IR slots).

## The change
1. `warroom/model.py`: put three facts in `app_data.json`: the league's IR slot count (Sleeper `roster_positions`,
   entries "IR"), each team's `reserve` list (Sleeper rosters), and each player's first week with a Sleeper
   projection above 0 (`pw`, already loaded; just the index).
2. `model.js` `decideAll`, free-agent branch: a player is "out now" when `proj(s, W0)` is 0 and he is `RES` or tagged
   Out/IR/PUP/SUSP. For him:
   - IR slot open (count > my `reserve` length): call **"Stash (IR slot)"**, no drop; `gains()` is run with no drop,
     since nobody leaves the roster.
   - No open slot: call **"Stash"** only when `dWin` ≥ 3 × `MIN_WIN` (0.009; Q-009 will replace the multiple with a
     measured noise floor), else "Skip", why "out until week N; not worth a roster spot yet".
   - `why` always says "out until week N (Sleeper projects him from week N)" and names the drop.
3. bids.json rows gain `back_week` (null for healthy players). Readers: `warroom/app.js` (`CLAIMS` set and the pill
   colours: Stash = amber, like Coin flip), `shq/guillotine.py` `v2_rows` (keeps the call lowercase), `docs/lib/core.js`
   bid header map (line 73: add "stash" → "Stash: out until week N"), `docs/war-room-v2/compare.js` (no change needed).
   The Q-050 rule that a guillotine fix names only players you can add now should treat a stash like a claim (never
   offered as a lineup fill-in).
4. Healthy free agents: unchanged.

## Test before shipping
- Unit tests (`tests/test_warroom.py`, Node fixture like the existing ones): an Out/IR free agent with `dWin` 0.018
  and no IR slot → "Stash", why names week N and the drop; with an open IR slot → "Stash (IR slot)", drop null;
  with `dWin` 0.005 → "Skip"; a healthy free agent's call unchanged.
- Replay on history: re-export every committed bids.json since Oct 31 (`git log -- docs/war-room-v2/bids.json`,
  in GitHub Actions; Node isn't on the laptop) and list every row whose call changes. Ship only if every changed row is
  an out-now player (expect Lamar Jackson on Nov 7-10), and no healthy row moves.
- Out of sample, after shipping: for each stash row, compare the drop's real points in the weeks the stashed player
  was out with what he scored after `back_week`. Not enough cases this season to gate on; report it in the next
  monthly review.
- health.json: no check should move (`wr.*` and `rec.*` don't score free adds). One-off Measure: no "Add free" row with
  injury Out/IR and `proj_next_week` 0.

## Risks and rollback
- Low. A wrong IR-slot count would offer a no-drop stash Sleeper refuses; the IR read is tested against the live
  league before shipping, and an unknown count means "no open slot" (the safe branch).
- A stud back in two weeks may now show as Skip if the floor is too high; the replay shows which rows move.
- Rollback: revert the `model.js` / `model.py` commit; readers ignore `back_week` and fall back to the call text.
