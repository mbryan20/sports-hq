# Q-041 · War Room v2 drops a usable RB to add a second DEF (or a kicker)

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-11. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
Two cases in two days, both from `warroom/model.js`:
- bids.json `f53bf00` (Tue Nov 10 7:37am CT): MIN DEF "Bid $1" (77%), drop Breece Hall (RB, 8.1 projected, 11.9 season
  average), while BAL DEF (8.5) and GB DEF (8.2) were free adds in the same file and CIN DEF (6.6) already starts.
- bids.json `d8b4c87` (Tue Nov 10 9:26pm CT): Spencer Shrader (K, 8.6 projected, +2.1 next week over Ka'imi Fairbairn's
  6.5) "Bid $1" (66%), drop Breece Hall again. MIN DEF had moved to "Priced out" (gain 5.2, $1 at 77%), next to BAL DEF
  "Add free" (gain 5.4).
Causes: `bestDrop` (lines 60-68) picks the player whose loss costs the fewest projected starting-lineup points over
all remaining weeks. My bench is four players at 7.8-8.3 (Tre Tucker, Woody Marks, Khalil Shakir, Breece Hall) behind
four starting RBs, so any of them reads as free: the lineup model gives no value to injury cover. And each row is
valued alone, so a paid claim at a position can stand next to a free add at the same position that does as much.

## The change
1. `bestDrop(t, s, skip)`: when the add `s` is a K or DEF, only consider drops that are K or DEF, or players whose
   average projection over `NEAR` is under `DEPTH_FLOOR` (5.0 pts). The natural drop becomes the K or DEF he replaces
   (Fairbairn for Shrader, CIN DEF for MIN DEF), and the add's gain is net of that loss. If no drop qualifies, the row
   is "Skip", why "no K/DEF or low-value player to drop".
2. `decideAll`, after `price()`: a claim (freed row with a bid) at position P is "Skip", why "<name> is free and about
   as good", when a free-agent row at P has `dWin` ≥ the claim's `dWin` − `MIN_WIN`. Same for "Priced out" rows (the
   page then says why it isn't worth chasing).
3. Not changed: drops for RB/WR/TE/QB adds (the lineup model is right that a bench WR behind four RBs adds little
   to a QB add; giving every bench player an injury-cover value is a bigger change, left for Q-012-style testing).
4. bids.json shape unchanged; `why` carries the reason. `shq/guillotine.py` `v2_rows` already drops Skips from the
   league page, and the Q-050 drop rules read `drop` as today.

## Test before shipping
- Unit tests (`tests/test_warroom.py`): a DEF add with a bench RB at 8 and a starting DEF → drop is the DEF; a K add
  with no K/DEF and no sub-5 player to drop → Skip; a paid DEF claim next to a free DEF with equal `dWin` → Skip with
  the free player's name; an RB add's drop unchanged.
- Replay (GitHub Actions, Node): re-export every committed bids.json since Oct 31 and list changed rows. Ship if:
  zero rows where a K/DEF add drops an RB/WR projected above about 8 (the Measure); no RB/WR/TE/QB row changes; every
  newly skipped claim names a free alternative whose `gain_rest_of_season` is within 0.5 of it.
- Out of sample: from the league's own past (`model.py` `calibration_cases`, weeks 1-9), count how often a rostered
  bench RB/WR above 8 projected later scored in a starting slot because of a bye or injury (the value the old drop
  threw away); report it with the build, so the 5.0 floor rests on this league's numbers, not a guess.
- health.json: no `wr.*` or `rec.*` check should move beyond its tolerance (`rec.*` scores only priced claims).

## Risks and rollback
- Low. A K/DEF add now costs its K/DEF, so a few small K/DEF gains drop under `MIN_WIN` and disappear: intended.
- If the roster ever holds two DEFs, the weaker one is the drop, which is right.
- Rollback: revert the one `model.js` commit.

S-M: about 20 lines in `model.js` (`bestDrop` filter, one pass after `price()`), 4 tests, the replay run.
