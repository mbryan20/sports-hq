# Q-075 · BTO and CFP cards: keep the posted pick unless the new one is clearly better; label each CHANGE

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-25. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
Week 12 (docs/picks/history.md, graded Tue Nov 24): the CFP card changed its pair in all four runs, mostly on 1-2 point
cover gaps. From `picks/data/card_versions.json`: Wed UCLA −3.5 54.2% + Arizona State 53.3%; Thu Tennessee 58.5% +
Texas Tech 55.2%; Fri Arizona State 55.6% + Ohio State 55.2%; Sat Texas Tech + Arizona State. BTO's third bet moved from
UCLA −3 (54.9%) to Texas Tech −3.5 (55.2%). The model is less calibrated than the market (`picks.brier_gap` +0.028,
n=444), so gaps like these are inside its error. Michael stopped acting on the CHANGE alerts that week, and this time
the ignored ones were right (BTO $3,080, CFP 23,808 pts). Two goals, both needed: fewer changes on noise, and a clear
signal on the changes that matter.

## The change
1. **Keep rule** (`picks/pp/cards.py`). The incumbent for each pool is Michael's saved bets when read
   (`slate[pool]["saved"]["bets"]`), else the last posted card (`data/published/{YEAR}-w{week}-{id}.json`, which
   `mark_changes` already writes). In `build_cfp_slate` and `build_bto_slate`, after `cands` are scored, an
   incumbent side keeps its slot unless a challenger's cover chance beats it by at least `KEEP_MARGIN` (new constant,
   start 0.03, chosen by the replay below), or the incumbent's game is locked or gone, or its cover chance fell under
   50%. Wagers and line points are re-sized for the kept package as today (`P.cfp_plan`, `allocate_line_points`,
   `P.bto_plan`). Yahoo is untouched (Q-061 already has its own keep rule).
2. **Label** (`picks/pp/push.py` `compare_ats`). Every BTO/CFP CHANGE line starts "must switch" (saved pick under 50%,
   locked-out, or beaten by 2× the margin) or "worth switching" (beaten by the margin to 2×), followed by the
   cover-chance comparison. `build_cfp_slate` gets the same `saved_vs_card` comparison BTO has (CFP lines today give
   only the card's reason).
3. Page: the CHANGE key in `READ_HTML` explains the two labels; a kept pick shows "kept: new best X is only +N
   points". The push's sound rule (urgent only within 24h of lock) is unchanged; "worth switching" never rings by
   itself on a draft.

## Test before shipping (out of sample)
- **Is a small gap noise?** On `2026_w1-8.json` (444 backtest games): bin pairs of candidate sides by their model
  cover-chance gap (0-2, 2-4, 4-6, 6+ points) and count how often the higher one covered more. Choose `KEEP_MARGIN` as
  the smallest gap where the higher side wins clearly more than half (expect 0.03-0.05); if no bin is above 55%, say
  so and set the margin from the CFP sizing sim instead.
- **Replay weeks 9-12** (`docs/picks/changes.json`, 4 weeks of versions, plus `card_versions.json`): rerun each version
  with the keep rule. Must show fewer pick changes per week (week 12 CFP: 3 changes → at most 1), summed cover chance
  of the final card within 1 point of today's, and graded results reported both ways (n is small; this is a sanity
  check, not proof).
- Health checks: `picks.card_ats` (0.692, n=117) must not fall below its tolerance band over the next 3 graded weeks;
  Q-071's override rows (when built) should show fewer differences per week.

## Risks and rollback
- Med. Too wide a margin keeps a worse pick (week 12 UCLA would have stayed); the replay bounds that cost before
  shipping. Labels could still be ignored; "must switch" is reserved for clear cases so it stays rare.
- Rollback: `KEEP_MARGIN = 0` restores today's behavior; the labels are text only.

## Effort
S-M: one constant and an incumbent check in two builders, a label in `compare_ats`, the CFP comparison, tests in
`tests/` with fixture slates (incumbent kept at +2, replaced at +4, replaced when locked), plus the two replays.
