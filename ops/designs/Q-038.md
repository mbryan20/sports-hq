# Q-038 · War Room v2 never credits a $0 bid with its payoff, so it recommends $1 that buys nothing

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-11. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
`warroom/model.js` `decideAll`, line 209: `let best = { b: 0, ev: 0, p: winChance(0, r.est) }`. The $0 option starts
with a payoff of 0 instead of (chance at $0) × value, so any $1+ bid with a positive payoff beats it, even when it
buys no extra chance. bids.json `d8b4c87` (last build before the Wed Nov 11 run): Saquon Barkley "Bid $1", value $3,
`win_curve` 0.57 at $0 and 0.57 at $1 (payoff $1.71 at $0 vs $1.14 at $1). Outcome (health.json Nov 11,
`recs.details.v2_rows`, leg 9): robert8063 won him at $2, so $0 and $1 both lost. Harold Fannin "Bid $1" (0.64; $0
was 0.57, payoff $1.71 vs $1.29): GoodFellas77 won at $1. Both $1 calls were wrong on the model's own numbers.
A second fault sits under it: `winChance(0)` counts every past claim that went for $0 as a win for me (line 126,
`b === 0 && x === 0`), but a $0 tie goes by waiver order. Fixing line 209 alone would lean harder on that overstated
57%, so both go together. (Q-039 is the wider fix for win chances that disagree with the rival list.)

## The change
1. Line 209: `best = { b: 0, ev: p0 * value, p: p0 }`, where `p0` is the $0 chance below.
2. `p0` = `winChance(0, r.est)` × my share of $0 ties: the fraction of rivals in `threats(s)` behind me in waiver order.
   Data: `warroom/model.py` adds each team's Sleeper `settings.waiver_position` to `app_data.json`. Missing data →
   share 0.5. (Not confirmed: that Sleeper breaks FAAB ties by waiver position in this league; check its settings
   page read-only before building.)
3. Call names: a priced claim whose best bid is $0 is "Bid" with `bid` 0 when `p0` ≥ 0.5, else "Coin flip" at $0.
   `docs/lib/core.js` line 81 already prints a $0 bid as "Claim at $0"; `warroom/app.js` gets the same text.
4. The budget loop (lines 222-233) reserves a drop for every claim with a call of Bid or Coin flip, not only
   `bid > 0`: a $0 claim also needs its own drop, or Sleeper fails it.
5. "Priced out" keeps its meaning (best payoff ≤ 0), which now only happens when `p0` is 0 and every bid overpays.

## Test before shipping
- Unit tests (`tests/test_warroom.py`): a flat curve between $0 and $1 → bid 0; a curve that jumps at $1 enough to
  pay for the dollar (p0 0.30 → 0.80 at value $3: $0 pays 0.90, $1 pays 1.60) → $1; Fannin's numbers (0.57 → 0.64,
  value $3) → $0 with a full tie share, $1 with half (the tie share decides close calls, as it should); two $0 claims with
  the same best drop → the second moves to its next drop; with waiver data missing, `p0` is half the ladder's.
- Replay on history (GitHub Actions, Node; the harness Q-025 describes): for each of the 9 priced waiver runs, ladder
  built without that run, compare the old and new bids against the real winning bids. Ship if: total surplus
  (value − bid when won) is at least the old rule's; no run is worse by more than $2; and the one-off Measure holds (no
  recommended bid with the same `win_curve` value as $0).
- health.json after shipping: `rec.win_brier` (0.17, watch, n=9) should not get worse; with the tie share it should
  move back toward 0.125 as $0/$1 rows stop claiming 57-64%. `rec.would_win` is watched, not gated (n is small).

## Risks and rollback
- Low. More "Claim at $0" calls, fewer $1 bids; with $5 spendable that keeps money for a claim that needs it.
- A wrong waiver order would mis-state `p0`; the 0.5 fallback limits the damage.
- Rollback: revert the `model.js` / `model.py` commit; readers already handle a $0 Bid.
