# Q-023 · Decide the college ATS stats weight (MODEL_W) on fresh weeks

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-09. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
College ATS cards center each game on the market spread plus 10% of the stats model's disagreement
(`picks/pp/cards.py:105` `MODEL_W = 0.1`, used at `:120` in `fair_margins`; the breakdown panel quotes it,
`pp/explain.py:420`). NFL already went to 0 (`pp/nfl.py:76`). The backtest (`picks/backtest/2026_w1-8.json`,
`report.model_weight`, n=444 college games) mostly says drop it:
- Walk-forward (each week's weight chosen on earlier weeks): weight 0 chosen in every scored week (3-8).
- RMSE weeks 1-8: 14.330 at 0 vs 14.401 at 0.1; best least-squares weight −0.107 ± 0.084.
- ATS picks where the model disagrees with the market by 3+ pts: 48.9% (n=274), below the 52.4% break-even.
- But the late weeks lean the other way: weeks 6-8 RMSE 13.676 at 0.1 vs 13.682 at 0; best weight 0.087 ± 0.236;
  3+ disagreements hit 54.5% (n=77). In weeks 6 and 7, 0.1 beat the market (14.330 vs 14.378; 13.345 vs 13.361).
- health.json `picks.brier_gap` 0.028 (model Brier 0.195 vs market 0.168, n=444).
So the early-season model is clearly worse, and the late-season signal is too small to tell. Weeks 9 and 10 have been
played since and are not in the backtest: they are the out-of-sample test.

## The change
Two steps; only the second touches the cards, and only if the test says so.
1. **Re-run the backtest through week 10** on the laptop (it needs the CFBD key): `py -m pp.backtest --through 10`
   writes `picks/backtest/2026_w1-10.json` with the same `model_weight` report. Point `cards.py:763` (calibration line)
   and `shq/health.py:532` (picks section) at the newest `2026_w1-*.json` instead of `2026_w1-8.json` by name.
2. **Set the weight from weeks 9-10 only** (never seen when 0.1 was chosen):
   - if RMSE at 0 ≤ RMSE at 0.1 on weeks 9-10 combined, and the walk-forward still picks 0: `MODEL_W = 0.0`, as NFL.
   - if 0.1 wins on weeks 9-10 and 3+ disagreements hit ≥ 52.4% there: keep 0.1, and record why in ROADMAP.
   - anything in between: keep 0.1 and re-check after week 12.
   With 0, `fair_margins` centers on market + news only, and the explain panel's line already handles `MODEL_W` = 0
   the way the NFL panel does (`explain.py:659`); copy that wording.
3. ROADMAP "MODEL_W stays 0.1" line updated either way.

## Test before shipping
- The weeks 9-10 numbers above, written into IMPROVEMENTS.md before the change, with n (about 110 games per two weeks).
- No pick on an already posted card changes (the change applies from the next draft).
- After shipping: `picks.brier_gap` is unaffected (it scores the raw model); the Measure is spread-pick ATS on the
  card once Q-024 splits `picks.card_ats` (BTO + CFP spread picks vs 52.4%), plus the next backtest's walk-forward RMSE.

## Risks and rollback
- Small either way: the center moves by a tenth of the model-market gap (a 5-pt gap = 0.5 pt), which matters only near
  a pool line's half point. Rollback: set `MODEL_W` back to 0.1.
