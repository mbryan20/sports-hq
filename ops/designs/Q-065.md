# Q-065 · NBA waiver swaps: a "+N this week" that means what it says

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-17. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
Two complete fantasy weeks are now judged (health.json, Nov 17 08:55Z, `sections.swaps`): 21 swaps shown, average
promise +7.63, actual −0.48, so `swap.est_bias` −8.11 and `swap.est_err` 13.32; `swap.gain` −0.44 z per add game
(weak, floor 0) and `swap.hit` 0.52. By week: ending Nov 8 bias −5.35 (n=8), ending Nov 15 −9.82 (n=13).
Where the miss comes from (tonight's read of `details.swaps`):
- Not missed games: adds played 48 of 49 expected games, drops 37 of 39.
- The add's per-game value: expected +2.40 z/g, delivered −0.57 (−6.63 per swap). The drop's: expected −1.03,
  delivered +0.21 (+1.48 per swap). So about 80% of the miss is the add being overrated: regression to the mean on
  fringe free agents valued on a handful of hot games.
- The size of the promise still ranks swaps (estimate vs result correlate 0.44), so the order is useful; the level isn't.
- **By league, the gap is all 3-Ball BB:** 10 swaps, bias −16.69, add ahead in 2 of 10, −4.33 z/g. Ball is Life: 11
  swaps, bias −0.32, ahead in 9 of 11, +3.09 z/g. Same 8 categories, similar waiver level (−3.63 vs −3.60). n=10-11
  each, so this split can still be chance; the design treats it as a hypothesis to test, not a fact.
Q-060's source trial (`sections.sources`) does not close it: DARKO minutes gains more per add game (+3.83 vs +0.89,
n=36) but misses more (10.55 vs 9.52), so its switch rule says not ready; shrunk values gain less (+0.45, n=21).

## The change
1. `shq/nba_swaps.py` `worth()`: for an **add** only, use a shrunk per-game value
   `v_add' = v_proj + (v - v_proj) × gp / (gp + K_ADD)`, where `v_proj` is his value on ESPN's season projection
   (the same projection the `shrunk` trial source already reads in `shq/nba_values.py` `alternative`/`shrunk_z`) and gp his games this season.
   Drops keep their value (they are mine; the data says they were underrated, not overrated). Start K_ADD from the fit
   in step 3, not a guess.
2. If step 3 shows the league split is real, a per-league factor in `config.py` (`NBA_SWAP_SHRINK = {"3-Ball BB": k1,
   "Ball is Life": k2}`), defaulting to one shared K_ADD.
3. Same guards, same order rules (Michael's rules 1-6 untouched); only the gain number and therefore the `MIN_GAIN`
   (+3) cut and the stream ranking change. The line says "+N this week (adds valued on a shrunk average)" in the
   existing `basis` text.
What Michael sees: fewer swap lines in 3-Ball BB (its 10 judged swaps promised +1.2 to +19.3 and 8 of 10 came out
negative), the same or nearly the same in Ball is Life, and numbers closer to what happens.

## Test before shipping (out of sample)
- Replay with `shq/nba_advice_backtest.py` (`compare_sources` already replays every morning since Nov 6 under a value
  source). Fit K_ADD on the week ending Nov 8, judge on the week ending Nov 15; then fit on Nov 15 and judge on the
  week ending Nov 22 once it is in. Leave-one-week-out on every week, not the pooled fit.
- Ship only if, on the held-out weeks: `swap.est_bias` moves from −8.11 to within ±3; `swap.est_err` falls below 11
  (from 13.32); `swap.gain` is not lower than the site's on the same mornings (the shrink must not drop the good
  Ball is Life swaps); hit rate stays ≥ 0.50.
- Per-league factor only if the held-out 3-Ball bias stays under −8 with one shared K_ADD.
- After shipping: the same four checks nightly; the health note's per-week lines show the first full week.

## Risks and rollback
- Fewer swaps shown; a real breakout free agent waits a few more games before he clears +3. Bench risers and the
  radar are untouched, so a breakout still shows there.
- Two weeks (21 swaps) is thin; if the week ending Nov 22 disagrees, keep measuring rather than ship.
- Rollback: K_ADD = 0 (no shrink) in `config.py`, one line.
