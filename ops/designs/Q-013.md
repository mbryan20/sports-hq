# Q-013 · Model health scorecard on the site

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-05. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
The nightly health job writes `docs/data/health.json` (Nov 4: 13 ok, 1 weak, 5 waiting, 4 info, 0 errors across 5
models), but no page reads it: `docs/lib/core.js` loads only `lineups`, `weekly`, `status`, `digest` and `pick_lines`
(`FILES`, line 9). Michael learned about the Nov 3 War Room findings only because he asked. Tonight's only flag,
`wr.cut_skill` (cut odds -1.7% vs a coin toss, n=8, floor 0), sits next to bid values he acts on every Wednesday, and
nothing on the War Room tile says so.

## The change
1. `core.js`: add `"health"` to `FILES` (one more small fetch, about 25 KB; the service worker caches it like the rest).
2. `core.js` new `S.healthHTML(d)`: one row per section (War Room v2, Our bids, Cut odds as shown, NBA radar, Pool
   Picks) with a status chip in the existing pill colours (ok = good, watch = warn, drift / weak / error = bad,
   waiting / info = muted), the section's notes, and a tap to expand its checks: label, value with unit, n, baseline or
   floor, and the date the baseline was set. Header line: "Checked <generated, CT>"; grey with "stale" if over 30 hours,
   reusing `footHTML`'s age rule.
3. Main page (`docs/index.html`, layout A): a "Model health" tile in "Pools and plans": "13 ok · 1 weak · 5 waiting" and
   the worst flag's label; tap opens the drawer with `healthHTML` (same pattern as the Today and Weekly tiles).
4. Where a model is shown, a one-line badge from the same file: the War Room tile and `/war-room-v2/` header
   (`warroom/app.js`, built by `warroom/build.py`) say
   "Cut odds: weak (no better than a coin toss, 8 weeks)" when `wr.cut_skill` is weak; the Picks tile shows
   `picks.brier_gap`'s status. Text comes from health.json's own labels, no new wording per check.
5. Owner: the dashboard redesign session owns `docs/lib/` and `docs/index.html`; build it there, beside the approvals
   card (`ops/approvals.md`, option A step 3) if Michael picks A.

No change to `shq/health.py` or health.json's format.

## Test before shipping
- `tests/test_health.py` already pins the JSON shape; add a check that every section has `id`, `title`, `status`,
  `checks`, `notes` (the page relies on them).
- Render check in the built-in browser at 375px and desktop, light and dark: the tile shows tonight's counts
  (13 / 1 / 5 / 4 / 0), the drawer lists all 23 checks, a section with `"status": "error"` renders red with its note, and
  a missing or 40-hour-old health.json shows "stale" instead of breaking the page.
- One-off success measure: Michael can say which models are flagged without asking (feedback button on the tile).

## Risks and rollback
- Over-trust in "ok": many checks are in-sample or n < 10. Show n on every check and the word "in-sample" where the label
  has it; waiting checks say what they wait for (from the section notes).
- Page weight: +25 KB per load; fine on phone data, and the 5-minute refresh already reloads the other files.
- Rollback: remove `"health"` from `FILES` and the tile; nothing else depends on it.

## Effort
S: about 60 lines of JS/CSS in `core.js` / `core.css`, one tile in `index.html`, one test.
