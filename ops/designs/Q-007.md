# Q-007 · Free-agent candidates are capped (3 per position, 24 total)

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-08. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
`warroom/model.js` `candidates()` (lines 159-181) takes the top 60 free agents by projected points (line 163), keeps
those that add at least 0.5 rest-of-season points (line 173), then keeps at most 3 per position and 24 overall
(line 177) before running the win-odds simulation (`gains`). Anyone 4th at his position is never simulated, so he can't
appear on the page, in bids.json or the Tuesday push. The old cap of 2 hid Bo Nix (Nov 3 audit).

Tonight's bids.json (built Nov 7 17:43Z, my FAAB $10, $5 spendable): 14 rows, QB 3, DEF 3, TE 3, K 3, WR 2. The cap binds
at four positions; the 24 total doesn't. 9 rows are "Add free" (win odds +0.003 to +0.015), 5 "Skip". With $5 left,
free adds are now most of what War Room v2 can do for me, and the cap decides which free adds I ever see.

## The change
1. `candidates()`: raise the per-position cap from 3 to 6 and the total from 24 to 40 (two constants at the top of
   `model.js`, `CAND_PER_POS` and `CAND_MAX`, so the test can set them). Freed players stay uncapped, as today.
2. Show: the page's table still lists the best 3 per position, with "+N more at QB" that opens the rest; bids.json carries
   every row (check during the build whether the Tuesday push text needs a length cap).
3. No change to the 60-player pre-filter, the 0.5-point gate or how a row is valued.

## Cost
Each extra candidate costs one `simulate` at `S.sims / 2` (3,000 seasons on the page, 10,000 for bids.json). Up to 16
more candidates is up to 16 × 10,000 more seasons in the GitHub build. Not confirmed: today's build time (Node isn't
installed on the laptop; measure it in the `war-room-v2.yml` run). The page computes on open, so check it on a phone.

## Test before shipping (out of sample: past snapshots, never tuned on)
- The 19 committed versions of `docs/war-room-v2/index.html` each carry the data blob `build.py` wrote (`__DATA__`).
  For each, run `export.js` with caps 3/24 and 6/40 (in a GitHub Actions job or with Node installed on the laptop).
- Count rows the old caps hid whose call would be Add free / Bid / Coin flip, and whose win-odds gain clears the noise
  floor from Q-009 (2 standard errors). Ship if at least one such row appears in the history or the cost is under 30
  seconds per build and 1 second on a phone; otherwise record "no hidden adds found" here and close the item.
- Same-seed check: the existing rows' calls and values are identical under both caps (the simulation seed is fixed).
- No health.json check covers it (one-off: hidden rows found / build time).

## Risks and rollback
- Slower page on a phone; the "+N more" fold keeps the table the same length.
- More near-zero "Add free" rows if the noise floor is too low: do Q-009 first or in the same session.
- Rollback: set the two constants back to 3 and 24.
