# Q-016 · A labelled "likely out (unconfirmed)" push when the news says a Questionable starter won't play

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-06. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.
**This changes the injury rule** (ROADMAP "Every injury call is cited": nothing is pushed on an unconfirmed status), so it
is Michael's call even more than usual.

## Problem
`reviews/2026-11.md` (Usefulness, Sun Nov 1): Breece Hall, Guillotine FLEX, was still Questionable on ESPN while its 8:07am
note said he "isn't expected to play". `status.confirm` (`shq/status.py:29`) keeps him at Q, and the injury watch only
plans swaps for starters whose status is in OUTISH (`swap.plan_injury_swaps`, `shq/swap.py:44`), so no push went out. The
site did show "Start J.K. Dobbins (7.7) over Breece Hall (0)" as a lineup move. In the guillotine league a likely zero is
the alert that matters most. One case so far, and no record of pushes to count more (Q-015).

## The change
1. New `shq/likely_out.py` `likely_out(p, now, week_start)` → the quoted phrase or None. True only for a starter whose
   status is Q (never None: a healthy player's news isn't read) when either
   - his ESPN note (`p["news"]["note"]`, refreshed by `run.better_news`) is dated this NFL week (Tuesday on, the same test
     `confirm` uses) and matches a short phrase list: "not expected to play", "isn't expected to play", "won't play",
     "will not play", "has been ruled out", "expected to miss", "will sit out"; and does not match "expected to play" without
     a negation or "not expected to play a full" / "a big role"; or
   - his league projection is 0 (or under 10% of his season average) while his team plays, inside 90 minutes of lock.
2. `shq/run.py` `injury_watch`: after the confirmed swaps, a second pass over starters in the 2-hour window
   (WATCH_LEAD_HOURS) with `likely_out`. The bench pick reuses `plan_injury_swaps`' rules (eligible, playable, strength).
   `push_once(key + ":likely", "<league>: likely out (unconfirmed)", "ESPN still lists Breece Hall as Questionable, but its
   8:07a note says 'isn't expected to play' [link]. Consider: bench him → start J.K. Dobbins (7.7 proj) at FLEX. Locks
   <kickoff>.")`. Never `execute_swap`, even where Sports HQ can write (Yahoo): Michael decides. One push per player per week.
3. Site: the row gets a "likely out (unconfirmed)" tag with the quote, and the Lineup moves line says why.
4. ROADMAP rule gets one sentence: an unconfirmed status may push a *labelled* "likely out" suggestion, never a swap.
5. Optional, same build: Doubtful starters (D is not in OUTISH, so they get no swap push today) use the same labelled push.

## Test before shipping
- `tests/test_likely_out.py`: the Nov 1 Breece Hall note → match; "is expected to play", "not expected to play a full
  workload", last week's note, a healthy player → no match; projection-0 path only inside 90 minutes.
- Shadow mode first (out of sample): for NFL weeks 10 and 11 (Sun Nov 15, 22) the pass writes what it would push to
  `state/likely_out_shadow.json` without pushing. Compare each with the inactives / box score: ship the push only if at
  least 4 of 5 "likely out" players really didn't play and none of the misses was a starter who played big minutes.
  No health.json check exists yet; once Q-015's push log lands, add `inj.likely_out_hit` (pushed players who sat).

## Risks and rollback
- False alarms from speculative notes ("may not play"): left out of the phrase list on purpose; the push is labelled and
  never swaps, so the cost of a miss is one extra push.
- Push volume on Sundays: one per player per week, only for starters, only inside 2 hours.
- Rollback: `LIKELY_OUT = False` in `shq/config.py`; the rule sentence comes out of ROADMAP.
