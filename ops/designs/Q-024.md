# Q-024 · Score spread picks and Yahoo straight-up picks separately

Drafted by the nightly improvement pass, 2026-11-06. Nothing here is built until the item's Decision says approved.

## Problem
`picks.card_ats` = 0.733 (22-8, n=30) is labelled "Posted card picks that won against the spread" and judged against the
52.4% break-even (`FLOORS`, `shq/health.py:71`). `picks_health()` (`health.py:531-558`) adds every pool's card picks into
one tally, and 25 of the 30 are Yahoo pick'em winners picked straight up (week 9: Yahoo 18-7, BTO 3-0, CFP 1-1; week 8 has
no card picks). The spread picks alone are 4-1 (n=5). So the one picks check that reads "ok" says almost nothing about skill,
and it is the Measure for Q-023. Entries are mixed the same way (32-27, 0.542).

## The change
1. `shq/health.py` `picks_health()`: split the tallies by pool type, as `picks/pp/grade.py` already does (`ats = pool in
   ("bto", "cfp")`, `grade.py:92`):
   - `picks.card_ats`: BTO + CFP card picks only (same id, label, floor 0.524 and MIN_N 10). `picks.entry_ats` (info): the
     entered spread picks.
   - `picks.yahoo_su` (info): Yahoo card picks won straight up. `picks.yahoo_edge`: card straight-up rate minus the rate of
     picking every market favourite on the same games ("higher", floor 0.0, MIN_N 20, tolerance 0.08).
   - Details keep `card` / `entered` and add `by_pool` (w/l per pool and side) so the wrap-up and Q-013 can show them.
2. The market favourite per Yahoo game: `picks/pp/grade.py` writes `fav` (team) into each Yahoo pick of the postmortem, from
   the slate's `market_home_spread` at the final card (`picks/data/cards.json`, the field `cards.py:181` reads). Weeks 8-9:
   a one-off backfill from `git show <final card commit>:picks/data/cards.json`. Not confirmed: that every Yahoo slate
   game carries `market_home_spread` there; if some don't, take ESPN's scoreboard odds for that game, else skip the game
   in both rates (never count it for one side only).
3. `CHECKS`: new labels; `picks.card_ats` keeps its id so Q-023 still points at it, but its baseline resets (Michael's OK at
   build time; the pass never edits `state/health_baselines.json`). It reads "waiting" until 10 spread picks (about week 10).
What changes for Michael: health.json (and Q-013's scorecard once built) shows a spread record against break-even and a
Yahoo record against "just pick the favourites", instead of one 73% that mixes them. No card, push or pick changes.

## Test before shipping
- `tests/test_health.py`: a fixture postmortem with 3 BTO, 2 CFP and 25 Yahoo graded picks gives `card_ats` from the 5
  spread picks only and `yahoo_edge` from the 25; a Yahoo game without `fav` is left out of both Yahoo rates.
- On the real files: week 9 must give `picks.card_ats` 4-1 (0.8, n=5, waiting), `picks.yahoo_su` 18-7 (0.72) and a
  favourites record on the same 25 games, checked by hand against the week 9 card.
- Out of sample: not a model change, so nothing to fit; the check is right when the hand count matches.

## Risks and rollback
- The picks section loses its only "ok" spread number for a few weeks (n=5 until about week 10): expected, and honest.
- A favourite that's a pick'em (spread 0): count it as no favourite and skip the game.
- Rollback: revert the commit; the old combined tally is the same loop without the split.
