## Problem
Health run Nov 6 17:43Z: `wr.cut_skill` −0.017 (floor 0.0, n=8, weak), `wr.best_sd` 36, `gd.dash_skill` −0.653 (n=1: week
8's cut team got 1.9%, 10th of 11), `gd.v2_skill` waiting (n=0). Rebuilt from Sleeper's projections, the real cut team
ranked 14, 11, 9, 1, 1, 11, 1, 10 by predicted risk (average 7.2 of ~14.5 teams) and scored 4 to 63 pts under its own
projection (average −35). A read-only replay tonight (weeks 1-8, `lowest_probs` from `shq/health.py`) adds:
- 36 is only the edge of the health grid (`warroom_cuts` tries 14-36). In-sample skill keeps rising to +2.7% at 45-55.
- Once the setting is chosen on the other 7 weeks (leave one week out), every fix loses to a coin toss: one wider swing
  −5.8%, the 22-pt odds shrunk toward equal odds −3.6%, heavier tails (Student-t, 3 df) −2.9%, a per-team swing of
  c × projection −5.7%. Choosing on earlier weeks only (weeks 4-8): −2.2%, −2.7%, −0.8%, +2.4%. The chosen swing jumped
  between 27 and 120 depending on which week was left out.
- If the 22-pt model were right, 8 cuts would score a log-loss of 2.35 (cut team ranked 4.7th on average); 2.71 or worse
  happens about 9% of the time by luck. So the odds are probably too sure, but 8 cuts can't pick the fix.
The models differ in shape: v2 uses one measured swing for every team (`D.sd.measured` 22.1 from `measure_sd`, n=116;
`swing(w)` in `model.js` adds 1.25/week); the dashboard sums per-position swings (`POS_SD` in `shq/guillotine.py`, about
27 for a full lineup). For week 9 they agree on order: bottom two 29.5%/29.5% (v2) vs 26.6%/23.0%, me 6.8% vs 7.2%.
Stakes are smaller than the item says: I have $10 FAAB ($5 spendable) and no FAAB bids are open (9 Add free, 5 Skip).
Cut odds now mainly drive the win odds shown, the Add free/Skip line (`MIN_WIN` 0.003) and the 20% danger push.

## The change
Step 1, test only (nothing on the page changes):
1. `shq/health.py` `warroom_cuts`: add 45, 55, 70 to the swing grid. New `warroom_cuts_lowo(lg)`: each week scored with
   the setting chosen on the other weeks, for two families: one swing (that grid) and the measured swing's odds shrunk
   toward equal odds by a (0 to 0.9). Also the forward-only score. Saved in `cuts_by_swing` as "lowo" (log-loss, skill,
   the pick for each week).
2. New check `wr.cut_skill_lowo` ("Cut odds vs a coin toss, setting chosen on other weeks"; higher, tolerance 0.05,
   floor 0.0, MIN_N 8): the better family. The `wr.best_sd` note says when the best swing is the grid's edge.
Step 2, only if the gate below passes (one setting, both models):
3. `warroom/model.py`: new constant `CUT_SD` (the median of the leave-one-out picks), sent as `D.sd.cut`. `model.js`:
   `S.sd` starts at `D.sd.cut` when present, else `SD_MEASURED`; the slider still marks the measured value.
4. `shq/guillotine.py` `entry()`: multiply `POS_SD` by `SD_SCALE = CUT_SD / 27` so a full lineup swings by `CUT_SD`
   (finished and in-progress starters unchanged).
5. If the shrink wins instead: apply it to next week's cut odds only (`exportBids` `cut_risk_next_week`, `analyze` `odds`),
   not the season simulation, so win odds stay as they are.
What changes on the page, bids.json and the dashboard: flatter cut odds (week 9 at a 36 swing: bottom two 21.6% instead
of 29.3%, me 8.6% instead of 6.7%); `settings.score_sd` shows the chosen swing next to the measured one; the dashboard
lands within a point or two of v2; the danger push fires less for the bottom team and a bit more for the middle.

## Risks and rollback
- Fitting noise: 9 cuts, picks spanning 27-120. The gate and the median guard against it but can't remove it.
- A wider swing flattens win odds and shrinks every player's added win odds, so more rows fall under `MIN_WIN` and Skip.
- The two models won't match exactly (the dashboard keeps per-player swings and locks starters during games).
- Rollback: drop `sd.cut` (model.js falls back to the measured swing) and set `SD_SCALE = 1.0`; step 1 is health-only.
